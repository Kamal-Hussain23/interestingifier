# MISSION.md

## What this project is

Interestingifier™ is a web app that turns a boring, everyday anecdote — missing the bus, burning the toast, getting locked out — into a hilarious, over-the-top theatrical story, and then narrates it aloud in an energetic Aussie accent.

You say your dull story out loud. Interestingifier™ gives you back an absurd version of it, told with joy and drama.

## Who it is for

Students who are just learning to program. The project must stay simple and approachable — small, easy-to-read code that anyone can follow. The aesthetic should feel confident and finished, never cheap or bare-bones.

## What we value

- **Take boring. Make it interesting.** Every plain story deserves an epic retelling.
- **Loud, joyful, eccentric-kitsch.** The app rejects clean, corporate, minimalist design. Leopard prints, neon, rhinestones, and playfully tacky visuals are not optional — they are the brand.
- **Simple over clever.** Prefer the most obvious solution. Small, readable code beats "professional" tricks.
- **Spec-driven.** No feature ships without a written spec first.
- **Production-ready from the start.** The inherited codebase should already feel polished; students grow it into a complete, working product.

## Non-negotiables

- Vanilla HTML, CSS, and JavaScript — no frameworks.
- A lightweight Python backend and simple SQLite persistence — no ORMs or heavy tooling.
- The signature three-step pipeline: transcribe speech, rewrite it as an absurd story, narrate it aloud.
- Never compromise the kitsch aesthetic for a "professional-looking" default.
